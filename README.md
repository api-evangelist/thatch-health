Thatch (Thatch Health, Inc.) is a modern health benefits platform that makes it easy for employers to offer personalized healthcare to employees using an ICHRA (Individual Coverage Health Reimbursement Arrangement). Thatch for Platforms is a set of REST APIs and embeddable components that let partner platforms — payroll, HR, and benefits products — bring ICHRA benefits to their customers, covering employer onboarding, employee management, enrollments, members, pay schedules, and payroll deductions.

**Areas:** Company · Healthcare · Health Benefits · ICHRA · Insurance · Payroll · Embedded Benefits · Human Resources

- [Thatch Health Deductions API](https://apis.io/apis/thatch-health/thatch-health-deductions-api/) — Payroll deductions track the costs of plan premiums to employees.
- [Thatch Health Employees API](https://apis.io/apis/thatch-health/thatch-health-employees-api/) — Employees work for employers, both of which are managed by partners. Employees enrolled in plans are also represented in Thatch as member objects.
- [Thatch Health Employer onboarding sessions API](https://apis.io/apis/thatch-health/thatch-health-employer-onboarding-sessions-api/) — Employers onboard into a platform through employer onboarding sessions. After creating a session, provide the `claim_url` to the onboarding iframe in your app.
- [Thatch Health Employers API](https://apis.io/apis/thatch-health/thatch-health-employers-api/) — Platforms onboard employers into Thatch, and have employees enrolled in plans.
- [Thatch Health Enrollments API](https://apis.io/apis/thatch-health/thatch-health-enrollments-api/) — Enrollments use member objects to track employee coverage status.
